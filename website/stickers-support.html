A pack of 206 animated stickers for Messages on iPhone and iPad.

Price and what's included

Pricing shown is for the planned public App Store release.

The full pack of 206 animated stickers is included in a one-time App Store purchase. The price in the United States is US$1.99. There are no in-app purchases.

In other countries and regions, the price shown in your local App Store applies.

Requirements

TensorSharp Stickers requires iOS 15 or later on iPhone, or iPadOS 15 or later on iPad. The sticker pack is used from within Messages. It does not require a separate account or an in-app purchase.

Find and use your stickers

  1. Open a conversation in Messages.
  2. Open the Messages app drawer or the + menu, depending on your system version. Find TensorSharp Stickers in the app or sticker selector.
  3. Choose a sticker to add it to the conversation. You can also touch and hold a sticker to place it on a message.

If the pack is not visible

Confirm that installation has finished and that you are looking in Messages. Reopen the conversation and check the app or sticker selector for TensorSharp Stickers. The location and appearance of these controls vary with the system version.

If an animation or sticker does not look right

Tell us which sticker is affected and what you see in Messages. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and the app version if available. A screenshot can help explain a display issue; share only the details needed to describe it.
