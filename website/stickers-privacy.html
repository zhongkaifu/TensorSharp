TensorSharp Stickers Privacy Policy

TensorSharp Stickers does not collect personal data through the app.

This policy applies to TensorSharp Stickers, the iMessage pack containing 206 animated stickers, and to support requests you choose to send us about this app.

Data handled by the app

The stickers are included in the app and displayed using Apple's native sticker-pack functionality. The app has no account registration, developer-operated server connection, advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data-collection SDK. It does not send your messages, contacts, identifiers, or sticker-use history to us.

Apple Messages and system services

When you choose to send a sticker, Messages and the operating system handle delivery to the recipient. We do not receive or store your conversations. Apple's handling of Messages, the App Store, and its other system services is separate from this app and is governed by Apple's privacy information and the settings you select on your device.

If you contact support

You may email fuzhongkai@gmail.com. If you do, we receive your email address, the message, and any attachments or technical details you choose to send. We use that information to reply to you and troubleshoot your request. Sending a support request is optional and is not required to use the stickers.

Retention and deletion

The app does not collect personal data, so there is no app-collected personal data for us to retain or delete. If you contact support, we retain your email and the information you provide only for as long as needed to respond to and follow up on your request, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing fuzhongkai@gmail.com. We will review and respond to your request and explain any information we need to retain for a legal obligation.

Scope of this policy

This policy describes the sticker app and its support correspondence. Visiting a website or following a link uses your browser and the services involved; those services may process information separately from the app.

Questions and updates

For privacy questions about TensorSharp Stickers, contact fuzhongkai@gmail.com. Changes to the app's data practices will be reflected in an updated policy and, where applicable, its App Store privacy information.
